\section{Functional Requirements}
\subsection{Module 1: Vision and Board Reconstruction}
\begin{description}
\item[FR-1.1] The system shall rectify the board to a canonical top-down image from four corners.
\item[FR-1.2] The system shall extract 64 square crops with a border margin.
\item[FR-1.3] The system shall output, per square, class probabilities and a visibility flag.
\item[FR-1.4] The system shall assign each frame a quality score and ignore frames below threshold.
\item[FR-1.5] (Iteration 2) The system shall detect and track the board automatically and re-detect on drift.
\end{description}
\subsection{Module 2: State Tracking and Move Inference}
\begin{description}
\item[FR-2.1] The system shall keep a trusted board that changes only by confirmed transitions.
\item[FR-2.2] The system shall confirm a move only when the post-move state is stable across a window.
\item[FR-2.3] The system shall score all legal move sequences (up to two plies) against observations and confirm only if the best exceeds the runner-up by a margin.
\item[FR-2.4] The system shall support captures, castling, en passant and promotion.
\item[FR-2.5] The system shall treat occluded squares as unknown, not empty.
\item[FR-2.6] The system shall enter UNCERTAIN on ambiguity or contradiction without changing the trusted state, and request manual correction on persistent conflict.
\item[FR-2.7] The system shall maintain castling and en-passant rights from move history.
\item[FR-2.8] The system shall accept a user-supplied correction as the new anchor.
\end{description}
\subsection{Module 3--4: Engine, Tutor, Application (Iteration 2)}
\begin{description}
\item[FR-3.1] Stockfish shall analyse only confirmed states. \quad
\item[FR-3.2] Moves shall be classified (best/weak/blunder) from evaluation change.
\item[FR-4.1] The app shall provide live tracking with overlay, PGN upload, video upload, history and analytics.
\item[FR-4.2] The app shall work in guest mode without an account.
\end{description}

\section{Non-Functional Requirements}
\subsection{Reliability}
NFR-R1: $\leq 1$ false confirmation per 100 stable/occluded events (target). NFR-R2: $\geq 90\%$ recovery from temporary failures. NFR-R3: a 30--60 minute session without unrecoverable crash.
\subsection{Usability}
NFR-U1: the user can always see system status (tracking, move detected, uncertain, manual correction). NFR-U2: setup and calibration without expert help. NFR-U3: usable by players of different hand size, handedness and skin tone.
\subsection{Performance}
NFR-P1: median confirmation latency $\leq 2$\,s after the board is stable (target). NFR-P2: sustained inference rate on the target phone to be measured. NFR-P3: engine analysis within a configurable time limit.
\subsection{Security}
NFR-S1: camera processing local by default; no raw video uploaded. NFR-S2: only derived data (FEN/moves) leaves the device, and only if the user opts in. NFR-S3: licences of all external components documented.
