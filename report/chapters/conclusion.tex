\chapter{Conclusion}
Chesstor targets state integrity in physical chess tracking. At the mid stage the core module is implemented,
unit-tested, and evaluated on simulated streams, where temporal confirmation plus legal-move constraints clearly
outperform frame-only and single-mechanism baselines, while stress tests exposed a genuine weakness to persistent
errors that we partly mitigated and will study with real boards. The principal gaps are the live phone interface,
real-board data and device latency. FYP-2 prioritises these, then the tutor features, robustness evaluation and an acceptance study.
