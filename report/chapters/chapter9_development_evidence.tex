\section{Security and Privacy Considerations}
Local processing by default; only FEN/moves may be stored in the cloud and only with sign-in; consent for any
recorded game; no raw video retained by default; session logs avoid personal data.
\section{Current Limitations}\label{sec:limits}
The live interface exists (web, and an Android packaging config) but has only been exercised with synthetic images and a
simulated feed, never a real camera or phone; the core runs on a laptop/server, not on the phone; video analysis, analytics and
accounts are not implemented (analytics shows sample data); no real-board data or results; observation limited to occupancy and colour (no
under-promotion); manual corners; assumes standard start and white at the bottom; classical classifier calibrated
per session; simulated noise may not reflect real error structure; one hand-occlusion model; latency not measured on a phone.
